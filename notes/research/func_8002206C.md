## Final causal change

population 0, schedule 3, allocation 1. Sharing that scratch with the mode-3
Y load had improved allocation, but was not evidence that the original source
shared those values. Redundant unsigned narrowing could clear block 10, but
was likewise unnecessary.

Replacing both table reads with the natural halfword-indexed pair was exact:


This removed the byte-address scratch from the deal loop entirely. Removing
the mode narrowing also remained exact. The prior local-allocation and
scheduler requirements were conditional on the reconstructed byte-address
operation, not requirements on all equivalent source expressions. No flag
override, assembly, register pinning, or artificial computation was needed.
